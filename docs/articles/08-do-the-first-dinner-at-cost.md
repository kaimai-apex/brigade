# Do the first dinner at cost

Jordan

The easiest start I know is friends and family. Tell them you cook private events, dinners, celebrations. Ask if anything is coming up.

Do the first dinner at cost. Groceries paid. Labor free. You get into the work. You build a referral network. You also find out if you want this life.

Document everything and put it on social. That is how you gather credibility and more clients.

Other routes exist. Private chef agencies will match you. Ads get your name out. I still say the biggest advice is know where your ideal clients already are. Affluent people. Affluent communities. High-end gyms. Sports car dealerships. Private jet places. Luxury real estate agents. Get in contact and offer your service as a gift they can give their clients.

You need food handlers, food safety, Smart Serve, a police check. You need a written contract: pay, how often you work, days off, what the client provides.

I am not telling you to stay free. I am telling you the first dinner is school. After that you charge. After that you know if the work fits what you want.

Restaurant chefs transitioning into private chef stall because they wait for a perfect posting. Text your aunt. Cook the birthday. Write down what you made. Post it with the faces blurred.
