# Dinner is at 6 until it isn't

Jordan

There is no set schedule.

Dinner can be set for 6pm. Things run late. The client has to attend something. The time slides back or it jumps forward. You roll with the punches and you make it work.

The version that tests you is four more people, one hour before dinner. That is the job. You can cook for them. Dinner moves another hour. You say that out loud. They need to understand that extra people come with extra cost.

Dietary needs stack more than they did five years ago. People are more health-conscious. Cooking one meal that covers everyone is hard. You still have to know how.

I do not think you need to be invisible. You need enough EQ to know where to stand. If the family is in a serious conversation, I am not next to them. If guests are over, I talk to the guests and make them feel at home. Knowing where to be and where not to be is a private chef skill.

Restaurant cooks hate a ticket that changes at the pass. In a house the ticket changes and there is no expo to yell at. You are the expo. Communicate the new time and the new cost, then cook.
