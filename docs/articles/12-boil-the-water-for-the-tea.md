# Boil the water for the tea

Jordan

The small things make their day easier.

If your client drinks tea after lunch, have water boiled so they do not wait. Anything they do not have to think about sets you apart. They thank you for that.

I look for the routine. Same tea. Same time. Same gap after the plates come back. You can stand in the kitchen like a cook waiting for a ticket, or you can already be ahead of the ticket they never fire.

This is the hospitality restaurants do not schedule you to learn. In a house there is no runner. You are the one who notices.

Four extra guests an hour out is the loud version of the same job. Boiling the water is the quiet version. Both say you are paying attention.

Restaurant chefs transitioning into private chef think the jump is the cooking. The jump is also the tea.
