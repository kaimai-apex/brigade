# I answer the door like I live here

Jordan

Discretion is a real part of this job.

A salesperson shows up. I act like I am the owner of the house. I do that to protect the client's identity and the family.

I do not post family photos. I do not post video where a face could give them away. On my socials I block out faces. If a face is in the frame I blur it. I ask permission if a face is going up. Addresses stay off the internet. Any hint that could let someone track them down gets covered.

You are always representing your clients in front of people they know. Bad etiquette travels. Showing up late. A bad attitude. Being disrespectful. Word of mouth is fast in this industry and it can hurt the business.

Every time I meet a client I put on my best self. Walk in. Smile. Shake hands. Greet everyone. Go above and beyond. That is how you get the next referral.

I do not vanish into the pantry for the whole service. I stay out of a hard family talk. I step in when guests need a host. That is the same skill as the door. You are in their house. You do not make their house public.
