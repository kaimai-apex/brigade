# Yacht work is for people who want to travel. I wanted to settle down.

Jordan

I have not done yacht work. I can still tell you who should.

People who like to travel, long hours, and adventure can take yacht cheffing. People with a spouse, a family, and a routine they want to keep will hate the demand and the seasons that jump around.

Household private cheffing is for people who want a stable routine and a paycheck. If you like talking to clients and building a connection, you will like the house. If you want more creative work and more autonomy, you will like private dinners and events.

I am in a settled season. I choose the household job for the stability.

Pay follows the structure you are in. Employed by the client means biweekly paychecks. Self-employed means you set it: per head or a day rate, cash, e-transfer, pay by bill. Contracts can run on or they can be seasonal. Dinners are usually a day rate.

Certifications I treat as baseline: food safety, food handlers, Smart Serve, police check.

I got the household job through Indeed and an agency. I got dinners through my own business. I reached out to people. Referrals started to stack.

I have not watched a private chef burn out in front of me. I am sure it happens. Unexpected requests you never agreed to will do it. Talk about scope and borders before you are already in the week.

Pick the lane that matches the life you are trying to build. I picked the house because I am building a family. The yacht can wait.
