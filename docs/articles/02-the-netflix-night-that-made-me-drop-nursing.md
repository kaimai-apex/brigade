# The Netflix night that made me drop nursing

Jordan

I am Jordan. I am 23. Chinese Canadian, born in Toronto, Ontario.

My first kitchen job was oyster boy. Shucking oysters. Cooking seafood. That was about it. Culinary did not look like a future to me. I went to nursing at Western Ontario.

After one year I dropped out.

One night in nursing I put on a Netflix show called The Final Table. They were combining flavors. They were using burnt flavors. Burnt is the thing you scrape off the pan. Bitter. Distasteful. Unpleasant. Usually it does not go well in a dish. They put it in and the dish was delicious. That is what got me. I wanted to know what else I did not know yet.

I left Western after first year and went to culinary school at Georgetown College. After that I worked restaurants in Toronto, Mississauga, Oakville. Then I went after private cheffing.

If you are still on a line and you think cooking is something you fell into, I get that. I shucked oysters and I still went to nursing. The show did not give me a career plan. It made me curious enough to walk away from a path I had already started.

I write this for restaurant chefs transitioning into private chef. That sentence is the whole point of Brigade for me. You can come from oyster boy. You can come from a year of nursing. You can come from a bunch of suburban restaurants. The jump is still available.
