# Start with groceries only. I underpriced myself on purpose.

Jordan

I have underpriced myself a lot. Imposter syndrome does that. You do not feel it in the moment. A couple years later you look back and ask why you charged that.

Underpricing is not always a mistake at the start. I tell new private chefs to charge grocery reimbursement and no labor, because they have no experience in the house yet. That is the first dinner at cost. School.

I felt like an imposter when I was transitioning. I wish someone had said I can do it. Clients care about hospitality and how you handle a situation. Restaurant cooks already have more than they think.

I knew I could go direct about a year in. People started asking me to cater. People asked for a private dinner.

Incorporation in Canada cost me about $200. Food handlers about $50. Payroll / accounting software about $100 a month. Admin is 1 to 2 hours a week: menus, schedules, paycheck stuff.

The hardest part of being the business is other people. Paying them well. Respecting them. Giving them enough information to grow their own careers.

You do not need a perfect rate on day one. You need a first table, a receipt for the groceries, and a note about what you would charge next time.
