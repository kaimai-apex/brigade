# Your first day is shopping, greeting people, and doing the clock yourself

Jordan

The thing I got most wrong in year one was time.

In a restaurant other chefs hold you up. Someone is on prep. Someone is on the pit. Someone already ordered the produce. As a private chef you cook, you clean, you plan the menu, you grocery shop. All of that sits in one day. I did not know how much time each task deserved. Fitting it into a schedule was the whole first year.

The first day showed me that. Holistic hospitality. Greeting the guest. Shopping. My own prep clock. My own cook clock. A restaurant chef does not walk in owning all of that.

I had a coach. I would not skip that if I were you. Mentorship is how I got my foot in.

After two months of doing it every day I stopped feeling lost. Mistakes taught me the timing. You cannot read your way into that.

If you are coming off a line, steal this as the system. Write the day in blocks before you shop. Menu. Groceries. Prep. Cook. Clean. Give each block a number of minutes and then watch where you blow it. That is the job they do not put on the posting.
