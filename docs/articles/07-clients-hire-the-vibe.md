# Clients hire the vibe. The food is on the resume.

Jordan

They will ask for your resume. They will ask where you worked. Some of them are checking if you vibe together. Being personable and connecting with the family is what I treat as the real interview.

Some clients want previous private cheffing. Some do not.

The food is a big part of the resume. The first impression is what decides it. Can you be hospitable. Can you connect. Can you sit with this family. That is what sets you apart from other chefs.

I staged for a private cheffing agency and I nailed the trial because I could talk to the family and I could execute. Station clean. Dishes. Multitasking. Identifying flavors. A stage is where you show the full skill set. Show it.

I got the job because someone was starting an agency and I was there. I also got it because I am personable. The cooking history was not the main reason.

I have seen chefs let go for misconduct. Treating staff and servers unprofessionally. Partying late. Showing up like the house is a bar.

Paperwork depends on the client. A lot of houses want a contract, an NDA, a background check, a police check. You are often around the kids.

If you are a restaurant chef transitioning into private chef, practice the hello as hard as you practice the protein. The plate gets you in the room. The vibe keeps you in the house.
